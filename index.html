Approach

How we build.

The same three steps, for every product, every time.

01

Design it properly

Every product starts with real thinking — what it does, who it serves, and why it deserves to exist. If we can't answer that, we don't build it.

02

Build it completely

Native apps. Real backends. No placeholders, no half-features. A flow either works all the way through, or it doesn't ship.

03

Stand behind it

Plain-language policies, honest support, and updates that keep coming. When something breaks, the people who built it fix it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does Veltravia Technologies do?+
We design, build, and run our own software products. Right now that's MarketScope AI (AI market analysis), Kora Messenger (private messaging), and VN Code (AI-assisted app building). Everything is built in-house.
Are your products free to use?+
MarketScope AI is free to start with a generous daily allowance, and a Pro tier for heavier users. Kora and VN Code are still in development — pricing will be announced with each launch.
Which platforms do you support?+
Android first — every product ships on Android, with iOS following. MarketScope AI is available on Android today.
How do I get support or report a problem?+
Email support@marketscopeai.com — we read everything and we answer. For anything urgent, put "Urgent" in the subject line.

Approach

How we build.

The same three steps, for every product, every time.

01

Design it properly

Every product starts with real thinking about what it does, who it serves, and why it deserves to exist. If we can't answer that, we don't build it.

02

Build it completely

Native apps. Real backends. No placeholders, no half-features. A flow either works all the way through, or it doesn't ship.

03

Stand behind it

Plain-language policies, honest support, and updates that keep coming. When something breaks, the people who built it fix it.

Company

Small by choice.
Serious by design.

Veltravia Technologies is an independent software company. We don't outsource the hard parts and we don't ship until things actually work.

Every product is built end-to-end under one roof: the design, the Android and iOS apps, the backend that powers them, and the support behind it all.

We'd rather build fewer things properly than many things halfway. That's the whole philosophy.

Craft

Every feature finished, every flow tested. Nothing ships half-built.

Honesty

Performance

Native apps and fast backends. Speed is a feature, not a bonus.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does Veltravia Technologies do?+
We design, build, and run our own software. Today that spans ten products: MarketScope AI (AI chart analysis for traders), Veltravia AI (our AI platform), Veltravia Wallet (self-custody crypto), Kora Messenger and Televa Messenger (private messaging), Numora (eSIMs and one-time numbers), StreamMax (movies and TV), MatchScope AI (multi-sport analysis), VN Code (AI-assisted app building) and the Nestora Realty site. Everything is built in-house.
Which products are available now?+
Two are live: MarketScope AI on Android, and the Nestora Realty website. Every other product on this page is in active build. Each card carries its real status, and we do not mark something live until people can actually use it.
Are your products free to use?+
MarketScope AI is free to start with a generous daily allowance and a Pro tier for heavier use. Pricing for the products still in development will be announced with each launch, not before.
Which platforms do you support?+
Android and iOS, mostly from one React Native codebase. MarketScope AI ships on Android today, Televa and MatchScope are Android-first, and the web products are plain HTML that loads without a build step.
How do I get support or report a problem?+
Email support@veltraviatech.com. We read everything and we answer. For anything urgent, put "Urgent" in the subject line.
